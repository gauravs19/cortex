What CORTEX does

Every number a proposal needs,
in one place

A 7-question wizard generates a full work stream breakdown. Then you refine it, cost it, and export it.

🗂️

Platform-specific stream splits

Choose your platforms — Web, iOS, Android, React Native, Desktop, API-only. CORTEX generates separate effort streams for each. No more bundling iOS and Android into one number.

⚙️

Backend broken down properly

Core services, integration layer, legacy adapters, and database design are separate streams. The complexity of your integrations (simple, medium, complex / legacy) scales each stream automatically.

💰

CapEx and OpEx clearly split

One-time project delivery cost (CapEx) and ongoing running costs (OpEx) are modelled separately. The client sees both: what they're paying for the project, and what they'll pay every month after go-live.

🛡️

Risk-adjusted contingency

Link to a CADEX deal to pull in the risk band automatically. Green deal = 10% contingency. Red deal = 35%. Or set it manually. The cost build-up shows exactly where the buffer sits.

📊

Full cost build-up

Base effort × daily rates, plus contingency, overhead, and target margin — each line visible. Editable rate card per role. Sell price calculated automatically from your margin target.

📅

Gantt timeline

Each work stream gets a bar on a Gantt chart. Phases (Discovery, Build, Test, Go-Live) are colour-coded. Sprint boundaries marked. Horizontally scrollable for long projects.
